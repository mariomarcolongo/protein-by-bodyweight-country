    References without visual noise

    One context, two endpoints.

    01

    Minimum

    The lower endpoint of the selected recommendation is drawn as a thin dashed line.

    02

    Upper end

    The upper endpoint is drawn separately. It is the top of that specific recommendation, not a universal safety maximum.

    03

    No universal protein upper intake level

    A general tolerable upper intake level has not been established, so the interface does not invent one.

    The hypocaloric athlete range remains explicitly based on fat-free mass and is converted only with a visible body-fat assumption.

    Built to be challenged

    The assumptions stay visible.

    01

    Protein is supply

    FAOSTAT reports food available at the end of the supply chain. It is not a direct survey of what each person consumed.

    02

    Weight is estimated

    The preview derives an adult proxy from sex-specific BMI and representative-cohort height. The next model will use age-sex population weights.

    03

    GDP is context

    The GDP plot adds economic context. Its cross-country correlation is descriptive, not a causal model.
